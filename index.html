WORDS FROM MY HEART

My dearest Janvi,

I don't know if words will ever be enough to explain what you mean to me, but I still want to try. You are someone who has a very special place in my heart. Even the smallest moments with you can become memories that I never want to forget.

I know we are not perfect. We have our arguments, misunderstandings, and moments when things don't go the way we wish they would. Sometimes, I get hurt. Sometimes, a few things make me feel a little sad, even when I don't express it properly.

There are moments when I wish you could understand what I feel without me having to explain everything. I don't expect us to be perfect, and I don't want either of us to pretend that everything is always okay. I just want us to understand each other a little better.

But even after those little hurts, my feelings for you don't simply disappear. I still love you. I still care about you. I still want to make beautiful memories with you, laugh with you, support you, and be there for you through the good and difficult days.

I may not always find the perfect words, and I may sometimes make mistakes too. But please know that you matter to me more than I can explain. I don't want a perfect love story. I want something real, where we both choose to understand, respect, and love each other.

Thank you for being a part of my life, Janvi. No matter how ordinary a day may seem, you have a way of making it special.

If there's one thing I want you to remember, it's this: even on the days when I feel a little hurt, there's still so much love for you in my heart.

Your Rawnit <3
